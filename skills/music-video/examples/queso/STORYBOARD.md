# Queso — storyboard, draft 1

Date night at a red-sauce trattoria. He counts her calories. She counts pepperonis. By the last chorus he is gone and the pizza is still there.

93 shots on the kick grid (98 BPM). Times are song time. **how**: lip-sync, motion and insert motion are Seedance clips; still is a Midjourney plate moved by the engine.
